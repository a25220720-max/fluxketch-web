3. Plans and payment

  • Free: every feature of the App is free for your first 3 DWG/DXF imports. Project file imports (including iCloud) and new drawings count toward the same 3; PDF imports and the sample drawing do not count. Once all 3 are used, you can view drawings and use the schedule, and the allowance does not refill. This rule applies from the release date of iPad and iPhone App version 1.3.1 and of the Galaxy app; when it takes effect, every free user receives a fresh 3 imports regardless of how many they had used before. (In iPad and iPhone App version 1.3.0 and earlier, 5 imports were available every 30 days from the first import; in 1.1.1 and earlier, up to 3.)
  • iPad plans: Lite provides drafting, editing, blocks, layouts, plot (PDF output) and DXF export; Markup provides markup, field pins, notes, reports and plot (PDF output), without drafting or editing; Pro provides everything in Lite and Markup. Paid plans include unlimited DWG/DXF imports. Monthly and yearly billing are available.
  • iPhone plan: provides the paid features on iPhone (creating pins, markup, markup PDF sharing, DXF export, plot, notes, reports, iCloud upload and more). Monthly and yearly billing are available. With an active Pro subscription on the same Apple Account, every feature on iPhone is included without the iPhone plan. Lite and Markup subscriptions do not unlock paid features on iPhone.
  • Galaxy tablet (Android) plans: the same Lite, Markup and Pro as on iPad, billed through Google Play (monthly and yearly). Galaxy phone plan: provides the paid features on a Galaxy phone (creating pins, markup, markup PDF sharing, DXF export, plot, notes, reports and more), billed through Google Play (monthly and yearly). With an active Pro subscription on a Galaxy tablet under the same Google account, every feature on the Galaxy phone is included without the phone plan. Lite and Markup subscriptions do not unlock paid features on the phone. Subscriptions from Apple (App Store) and Google Play do not transfer to each other — a subscription bought on iPad or iPhone does not unlock features on Galaxy, and vice versa.
  • No free trial (a trial period followed by automatic billing) is currently offered.
  • Subscription products and prices are shown on the in-app purchase screen. The actual amount and currency are set by Apple or Google according to your App Store or Google Play region.
  • Payment is charged to your App Store account (Google Play account on Galaxy) when you confirm the purchase.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically. Unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends, the next period is charged at the same price.
  • You can cancel anytime in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPad or iPhone, or from the Subscription screen (Plan on iPhone) in the App's settings; on Galaxy, in Play Store › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions or Manage · cancel subscription in the App's settings. Cancelling stops the next charge only; you keep access until the end of the period already paid for.

4. Cancellation and refunds

  • Apple is the seller of in-app purchases, and Apple handles payments and refunds. The Company cannot issue refunds directly.
  • You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple decides according to its own policies.
  • Under Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, you may withdraw a purchase within 7 days of payment. However, under Article 17(2)(5) of that Act, withdrawal may be limited for digital content whose value has been substantially reduced by use (for example, if you actually used paid features during the subscription period). Even then, withdrawal is possible for the unused portion.
  • Galaxy (Google Play): Google Play handles payments. Within 48 hours of purchase you can request a refund directly from Google Play (Google Play refund guide); after that, email flux0720@fluxketch.com and the Company will review the request and refund through Google Play.
  • Changing plans on Galaxy (Google Play): switching to a higher plan takes effect immediately. If the daily price goes up, you are charged only the difference for the days remaining in the current billing period, and your billing date stays the same. If the daily price goes down — for example, monthly → yearly on the same plan — you pay the new plan's price and the time remaining on your current plan is added to the new plan's period. Switching to a lower plan takes effect after the current billing period ends.
  • When a refund is approved, the subscription entitlement is removed from the App automatically.
  • If you could not use the service because of the Company's fault, email flux0720@fluxketch.com and we will guide you through Apple's refund process.
