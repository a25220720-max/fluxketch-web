Field notes

What you saw, left on the drawing.

Photo pins, handwritten markup and note pages live in one file. Positions stay put across modes, and they collect into a single report.

From pin log to report

Drop a pin, attach a photo and a note. The pin log is the list; the report prints in seven document formats (inspection, instruction, meeting and more).

  • Pins always sit above markup, locked to drawing coordinates
  • Table layouts and regulatory reference lines in reports
  • Sheet issue exports just the sheets you choose

Handwriting that stays handwriting

Apple Pencil pressure as-is. Strokes never split from the drawing, and widths are in drawing units, so meaning survives a scale change.

  • Pen, marker, pencil, eraser, lasso, ruler
  • Undo/redo, ink plotted as-is
  • Note pages: blank, ruled, dotted, grid, drafting grid

See at true scale, measure with a ruler

Per-device screen density puts 1:50 on screen at 1:50. A 100 mm ruler on paper verifies even the printer's scaling.

  • 1:1 to 1:1,000 · calibrate against a ruler
  • DXF asks the unit and corrects on read (mm, cm, m, inch, ft)
  • PDF underlays scale with two points and snap to vectors

Frequently asked questions

Where do I get it?
Search the App Store for "Fluxketch" or use the Download button above. It is an iPad app.
Is it free?
Opening and viewing drawings is free forever. Drafting, markup and export are Lite ($2.99/mo, $29.99/yr); pins and reports are Pro ($7.99/mo, $79.99/yr). Right now Lite is free until the October 14 full release, and monthly plans start with a 7-day free trial.
Which files can it open?
DXF, DWG (server conversion) and PDF (underlay). Layers, colors, weights, linetypes, blocks, hatches, multilines, 3D faces and leaders are imported; anything that isn't gets counted in the import report.
Does it work offline?
DXF and PDF work fully offline. Only DWG needs the conversion server, so it needs a connection (save as DXF on a PC to work offline).
Is there 3D or AI?
No. Fluxketch focuses on opening, drawing, marking up and exporting 2D drawings on site.
What hardware do I need?
An iPad and an Apple Pencil. Large drawings are most comfortable on M-series iPads, and we also verify on iPad 9th gen and A16 models.
Are markups saved with the drawing?
Yes. Markup, pins and notes are stored inside the drawing file in drawing coordinates. Plot PDFs carry the ink and pins, and DXF export is available.
How do I cancel?
In Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad, anytime. You keep access until the period ends, and cancelling a free trial at least 24 hours before it ends means no charge.
Where do I report a problem?
Use the app's 'Report a bug now' card or email flux0720@fluxketch.com with the file. Instagram @fluxketch_official works too.
