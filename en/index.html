One drawing holds the whole site

CAD geometry, handwritten markup, photo pins and note pages live in one file. Switch modes and everything stays in place.

Open

DXF · DWG · PDF

DXF is parsed on device and asks for its unit. DWG goes through a conversion server. PDF lays down as an underlay at a chosen scale (1:100 by default) — and snapping works on its vector lines.

Draw

12 CAD tools

Line, polyline, circle, arc, rectangle, text; move, copy, fillet, trim, erase, dimension. Endpoint, midpoint, center and intersection snaps, ortho, and a numeric keypad.

Mark

Markup · Pins · Notes

Apple Pencil markup is anchored to drawing coordinates, so it stays put when you zoom and pan. Drop pins with photos and memos, and write note pages in the same file.

View

60Hz on big drawings

Metal vector rendering plus a raster tile pyramid. A 180,000-entity drawing pans and zooms at frame rate and sharpens the moment you stop.

Export

Scaled PDF · DXF

A4/A3 plots at 1:50, 1:100, 1:150 and more, window selection, and a 100 mm verification bar on the paper. DXF round-trips the v1 drawing set losslessly (R2000).

Report

Pin report PDF

Collect the pins on a drawing into one report — cover, list, photos and notes — so defects, instructions and questions travel with their exact location.

Snapping holds your hand

Hover over geometry with a tool armed and the endpoint, midpoint, center and intersection snaps preview before you touch. Turn on ortho and every line is square.

  • 15 pt snap radius; hover preview on supported iPads
  • Erase with one tap, or draw a fence and lift to delete everything it crossed
  • Dimension: two taps, real length with units

Markup stays on the drawing

Pen, marker, pencil, eraser, lasso and ruler. Strokes are fixed to drawing coordinates so they never drift from the lines beneath while you zoom or pan. Widths are defined in drawing units, so a scale change keeps their meaning.

  • Pins always render above markup
  • Sheet view (black on white) for print-like review
  • Markup ink is included in plots

Note pages in the same file

Blank, ruled, dotted, grid and drafting grid on white, cream or black paper. Pull past the end and a new page appears. Meeting notes and drawings stay in one project.

  • Landscape and portrait — in portrait the top actions move to a right-hand column
  • Canvas lock to freeze the screen while you show it
  • A 28-step practice board teaches the first session
