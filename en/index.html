A-101 · Features

Open. Fix. Mark up. Export.

Take office drawings out as they are, and send what you touched on site straight back.

01 Open

Drawings as they are

DWG, DXF and PDF with layers, colors, blocks and hatches intact. Smooth even at 1.4M entities.

02 Fix

Draft right on site

12 CAD tools — line, circle, arc, dimension and more — with endpoint and ortho snaps and a numeric keypad.

03 Mark up

Markup and photo pins

Handwriting locked to drawing coordinates with pencil pressure, pins with photos and notes, note pages.

04 Export

Out at true scale

Plot PDF, DXF export, reports and sheet issue. From site to office, right away.
