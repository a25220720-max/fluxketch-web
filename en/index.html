See it like the print

One tap turns the canvas into black lines on white paper. No old colors bleed through while you pan and zoom, and hidden layers are excluded by the same rule on screen, in snapping, in reports and in plots.

  • Drawing colors or the black-on-white sheet, one tap apart
  • Vectors stay crisp at any zoom
  • The plot PDF ships this sheet as is, with the scale and a 100 mm verification bar

A-301 · Work

One drawing holds the whole site

CAD geometry, handwritten markup, photo pins and note pages live in one file. Switch modes and everything stays in place.

Measure, snap, fit

The dimension tool shows the real length with units after two taps. Hover over geometry with a tool armed and the snap points preview; turn on ortho and every line is square.

  • DXF asks for its unit and corrects coordinates as it reads (mm, cm, m, inch, ft)
  • PDF underlays start at 1:100; two taps set the true scale, and snapping works on PDF vector lines
  • Plots carry a 100 mm bar on paper, so even printer scaling is checkable

188 layers, intact

Visibility, lock, color, weight and linetype live in the left palette. A hidden layer does not just leave the screen; snapping, reports and plots drop it by the same rule.

  • Metal vector rendering plus a raster tile pyramid: 60 Hz pan and zoom on 180,000 entities
  • Sharpens the moment you stop (118 ms on a 46,529-entity drawing)
  • Erase, hide, line and dimension show within 100 ms

Markup stays on the drawing

Pen, marker, pencil, eraser, lasso and ruler. Strokes are fixed to drawing coordinates so they never drift from the lines beneath while you zoom or pan. Widths are defined in drawing units, so a scale change keeps their meaning.

  • Pins (photo, memo) always sit above markup; collect them into one report PDF
  • Markup ink is included in plots
  • Note pages in the same file: blank, ruled, dotted, grid, drafting grid; pull past the end for one more

Topographic maps open too

City parcels and mountain contour lines on one sheet. Hundreds of thousands of entities still open, hold the frame rate while you pan and zoom, and sharpen the moment you stop. The raster tile pyramid bakes base tiles on first open and covers from disk after that.

  • Measured on a 181,422-entity drawing: opened and edited, peak memory ≈1.0 GB
  • Dense section and detail sheets stay intact (Nha Trang hotel sections)
  • DWG through the conversion server, DXF directly on device

Sections and details, in their colors

Layer colors, linetypes, hatches and dimension text arrive as drawn. Scan six details inside the sheet border on one screen and zoom only where you need to.

  • ACI color, linetype and weight round-trip (R2000)
  • Screen-constant line weight keeps lines crisp when zoomed out
  • Flip to sheet view for black lines on white

Landscape and portrait

Turn the iPad and undo, save and the menu move to a right-hand column while keypad, lock, ruler, dimension and pin move to the top right. The left palette and the right snap rail stay put.

  • Verified on five iPads from mini to 13-inch, both orientations
  • Canvas lock freezes the screen while you show it
  • A 28-step practice board and a built-in sample plan teach the first session
