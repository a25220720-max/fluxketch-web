Questions and answers

Questions about drawing markup

Which markup tools are there?

Pen, marker, pencil, eraser, lasso and ruler. Apple Pencil pressure is used as-is, and color and width come from the palette. Undo and redo apply to markup too.

Do strokes drift from the drawing when I zoom?

No. Strokes are stored in drawing coordinates, not screen pixels, and widths are in drawing units. They move with the drawing through zoom and pan, and a separate input surface keeps existing ink steady while you draw.

Fingers versus Pencil?

Fingers pan; Apple Pencil draws. The Pencil never pushes the canvas. In one-finger pan mode, lasso, eraser and move still work with the Pencil.

How do I attach photos?

Drop a pin and add a photo and a note. Pins always draw above markup and are locked to drawing coordinates. The pin log is the list, and reports print photo, note and position together.

Can I take separate notes?

Add note pages — blank, ruled, dotted, grid, drafting grid — inside the drawing file, so meeting notes and sketches live with the drawing.

Does markup show up in office CAD?

Markup ink and pins print into plot PDFs and reports. DXF export carries CAD entities; handwriting travels as PDF, which matches how sites actually work.

Can I mark up a PDF drawing?

Yes. Open the PDF as an underlay, set the scale with two points, and mark up, pin and draft on top.

Sheet view or CAD mode?

Both. On white sheet view you write as if on a print; in dark CAD mode you write over the layer colors. Strokes stay put when you switch.

How it works

From markup to report

  1. Enter markup

    Tap the Markup tab at the bottom to open pen, marker, pencil, eraser, lasso and ruler.

  2. Draw and pin

    Mark with Apple Pencil, then add photos and notes to pins.

  3. Make the report

    Choose Report in the More menu and print in seven document formats — inspection, instruction, meeting and more.
