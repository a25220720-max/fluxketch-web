This is an English translation for convenience. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

This policy describes what the Fluxketch app actually does, based on its code. We do not collect personal information such as your name, contact details or account (the Galaxy free-import check is described in section 2). Your drawings, markups, photos and notes stay on your device (iPad, iPhone, Galaxy tablet or phone) and, if you use it on iPad or iPhone, in your own iCloud. The Galaxy (Android) app does not use iCloud. If anything changes, we will announce it on this page and in the app 7 days before it takes effect.

1. No personal information goes to our servers (except the Galaxy free-import check)

The Fluxketch app contains no third-party SDKs such as analytics, crash reporting or advertising, and does not send usage records to the developer's servers (the Galaxy free-import count is the only exception — see section 2). It does not use advertising identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, Android advertising ID), location or contacts.

3. What is stored on your device

  • Drawing projects (.fluxproj), markups, pin photos, notes and voice memos, note pages and report PDFs are all stored in the app's Documents folder on your device. On iPad and iPhone they are visible and copyable in the Files app, and a project goes to iCloud only when you tap "Upload to iCloud" (see the table above). On Galaxy they are stored in the app's private storage; to take them out, use the app's export (DXF, PDF) or share.
  • The default author name on pins is the device name (for example, "Kim's iPad" or "Galaxy Tab S10 FE"). It goes out in report PDFs and shared packages together with names written in pins and comments. Please check before exporting.
  • Pin photos are re-saved as JPEG with EXIF and GPS information removed.
  • Subscriptions are handled on the device by Apple StoreKit on iPad and iPhone, and by Google Play Billing on Galaxy. Only Apple or Google handles payment information.

4. Permissions

Camera and Photos (pin photos), Microphone and Speech Recognition (voice memos), Local Network (finding a conversion server). iPadOS or iOS asks for each permission the first time you use that feature, and you can keep using everything else if you decline.

Galaxy: the app asks only for the Microphone (voice memos). Pin photos are taken with the Android camera app or chosen in the photo picker, and the app receives only the photo you pick, so it does not request Camera or Photos permission.

5. Sending feedback

"Send feedback" in the app opens Google Forms. What you write in the form and what Google collects are subject to Google's privacy policy. We use form responses only to improve the product and reply to you, and delete them once we have replied.

6. Subscriptions and payment

On iPad and iPhone, Apple handles subscription payments, renewals and refunds; on Galaxy, Google Play does. We never receive or store payment information such as card numbers. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your iPad or iPhone, and request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Galaxy, manage or cancel in Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. See the Terms of Use for details.
