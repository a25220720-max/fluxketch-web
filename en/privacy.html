Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026 · Revised October 12, 2026 · Based on what the app code actually does · 한국어

This is an English translation for convenience. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

This policy describes what the Fluxketch app actually does, based on its code. We do not collect your personal information. Your drawings, markups, photos and notes stay on your device (iPad, iPhone) and, if you use it, in your own iCloud. If anything changes, we will announce it on this page and in the app 7 days before it takes effect.

1. No personal information goes to our servers

The Fluxketch app contains no third-party SDKs such as analytics, crash reporting or advertising, and does not send usage records to the developer's servers. It does not use device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV), location or contacts.

2. Data that leaves the app

WhenWhatWhere
When you import a DWG drawingThe original bytes of that DWG fileThe conversion server you set, or the app's default conversion server (HTTPS). The file is deleted from the server as soon as conversion finishes. If you set a PC on the same Wi‑Fi as the server, it may be sent over plain HTTP.
When you turn a voice memo into textThe recorded audioApple speech recognition (Apple servers). Apple's privacy policy applies.
When you tap "find a conversion server" on the same Wi‑FiLocal network discovery requestsYour local network (only when you tap the opt-in button)
When you tap "Upload to iCloud" or "Import from iCloud"That entire project fileYour own iCloud Drive (the app's folder, Apple). It does not pass through our servers.
When you use the work schedule and daily notes (automatic if you are signed in to iCloud)Schedule items, names, colors and dates; the text and handwriting of daily notesYour own iCloud (the app's private space, Apple), to keep your other iPads and iPhones on the same Apple Account in sync. It does not pass through our servers, and you can turn it off in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud on your device.
When the app checks for a new versionThe app identifier (bundle ID) onlyApple App Store lookup (itunes.apple.com). No personal information is sent.

3. What is stored on your device

  • Drawing projects (.fluxproj), markups, pin photos, notes and voice memos, note pages and report PDFs are all stored in the app's Documents folder on your device and are visible and copyable in the Files app. A project goes to iCloud only when you tap "Upload to iCloud" (see the table above).
  • The default author name on pins is the device name (for example, "Kim's iPad"). It goes out in report PDFs and shared packages together with names written in pins and comments. Please check before exporting.
  • Pin photos are re-saved as JPEG with EXIF and GPS information removed.
  • Subscriptions are handled on the device by Apple StoreKit. Only Apple handles payment information.

4. Permissions

Camera and Photos (pin photos), Microphone and Speech Recognition (voice memos), Local Network (finding a conversion server). iPadOS or iOS asks for each permission the first time you use that feature, and you can keep using everything else if you decline.

5. Sending feedback

"Send feedback" in the app opens Google Forms. What you write in the form and what Google collects are subject to Google's privacy policy. We use form responses only to improve the product and reply to you, and delete them once we have replied.

6. Subscriptions and payment

Apple handles all subscription payments, renewals and refunds. We never receive or store payment information such as card numbers. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your iPad or iPhone, and request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. See the Terms of Use for details.
